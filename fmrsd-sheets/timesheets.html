◀ ▶ change the month; each month is its own sheet. Type the day and the month fills in. Enter or ↓ moves down a column, Tab moves across. Bus numbers carry over from month to month.
Times: type 645, 6:45, 230p, 7:30pm. With no a/p, 6–11 means AM, 12 is noon and 1–5 is PM. An Out earlier than its In is read as later in the day. Enter or ↓ moves down a column, Shift+Enter or ↑ moves up, Tab moves across. With the times beside it blank, Total (or Hours Worked) takes hours typed in directly. School columns take hours (1.5 or 1:30); Hours Worked turns red if they don't add up to it. Entries save automatically to meercat.
